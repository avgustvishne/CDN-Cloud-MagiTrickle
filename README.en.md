## Provider subscriptions vs ASN subscriptions

- **Provider subscriptions** — ready-to-use IP/CIDR lists generated from available sources. They generally provide broader coverage of a specific provider.
- **ASN subscriptions** — IP/CIDR ranges belonging to a specific Autonomous System Number (ASN). They are useful for selecting a specific network or infrastructure.

**In short:** provider subscription — **broader provider coverage**; ASN subscription — **a specific provider network**.
